Common questions

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Open the app, go to Profile > Settings > Delete My Account. This removes your profile, drops, comments, likes and agreement record from PulseDrop. If you can't open the app, email us from the address you use and we will do it for you.

How do I download a copy of my data?

Profile > Settings > Export My Data.

How do I delete one of my drops?

Tap the ••• button on your drop and choose Delete Drop, or press and hold the drop on your Profile.

How do I report a drop or block someone?

Tap the ••• button on the drop and choose Report Drop or Block. You can also tap a person's name to report, block or add them to your Private Circle.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check Settings > Notifications > PulseDrop on your iPhone, and Profile > Settings > Circle & Remix Alerts in the app. You only get alerts for people in your Private Circle, likes on your drops, and remixes of your vibes.

I can't see new drops.

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud (iPhone Settings > your name) and pull down on the feed to refresh.

About the songs

Songs are 30-second previews from Apple. They link back to Apple Music, and PulseDrop does not copy or host the audio.
