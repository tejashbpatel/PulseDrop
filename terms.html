Terms & Community Rules

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Community Rules

PulseDrop is for sharing real moments, sounds and vibes. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You agree not to post:

  • Nudity, sexual content, or anything involving minors in a harmful way
  • Harassment, hate speech, threats, or bullying
  • Violence, dangerous acts, or self-harm content
  • Spam, scams, or impersonation
  • Content that you do not have the right to share

Reporting and Blocking

Every drop has a menu (•••) where you can report it or block its author. Reported drops are hidden for you immediately and reviewed. Content that breaks these rules is removed within 24 hours, and the accounts responsible may be removed.

Your Content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you allow PulseDrop to store and show it to other users of the app. You can delete your own drops at any time from the drop menu. Songs you add are 30-second previews from Apple. They belong to their artists and labels, are not copied by PulseDrop, and link back to Apple Music.

Your Account and Data

PulseDrop uses your iCloud account to save your drops and profile. Your profile name, username, bio, photo, and drops are visible to other users. Your photos and microphone are only used when you choose to attach them to a drop. You can delete your account and all of your content at any time from Profile > Settings > Delete My Account, and download a copy of your data from Settings > Export My Data.
